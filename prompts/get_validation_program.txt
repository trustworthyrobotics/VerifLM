Rules:

1. Use only whole-object contacts and whole-object XYZ relations. Do not refer to semantic parts such as handles, rims, slots, pegs, or spouts.

2. `required_contacts` must exist after settling, or at the proposed pose for `geometric_only`. `forbidden_contacts` must not occur. Use empty lists when no contact constraint is clearly implied.

4. For `settling`, choose drift tolerance based on how much motion is acceptable while still preserving the intended goal:
